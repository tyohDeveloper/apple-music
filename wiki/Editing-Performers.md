# Editing performers

Performers are the people and collectives credited in your library: persons, groups and bands, duos, orchestras, choirs and ensembles. Open a library and choose **Performers**.

## Finding a performer

Search matches any of a performer's names, ignoring case, accents and punctuation: "beyonce" finds "Beyoncé", and a birth name finds the performer it belongs to. Results are ordered by sort name, 25 to a page.

## Creating a performer

Choose the kind, enter the name, and optionally a sort name such as "Beatles, The" or "Cash, Johnny". The name becomes the performer's preferred name, the one shown everywhere.

## Names

A performer can have any number of names, each with a kind (primary, legal, birth, stage, alias, spelling variant, former, transliteration, abbreviation), an optional language tag such as `ja-Latn`, and optional dates it was in use.

- **Make preferred** shows the performer under that name from then on.
- A performer always has exactly one preferred name, so the preferred name cannot be removed; make another name preferred first.
- A changed name is recorded as a new name with dates, not as a new performer, so credits keep the name used at the time.

## Relations

A relation says how two performers stand to each other: member of, conductor of, music director of, subgroup of, persona of, successor of, or collaborator with. Find the other performer, choose the relation as read from the performer you are editing ("John Lennon is a member of The Beatles"), and optionally list roles or instruments. The relation appears on both performers' pages; each name is a link.

## Dates

Dates can be a year, a year and month, or a full date: `1969`, `1969-07` or `1969-07-20`. Impossible days such as `1933-02-30` are refused.

## Deleting

**Delete performer** asks for confirmation, then removes the performer with its names and relations. A performer that is still credited cannot be deleted, and neither can a name a credit uses; the **Credited on** list at the foot of the page shows where the performer is credited.
