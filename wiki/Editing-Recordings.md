# Editing recordings

A recording is one captured performance: a studio take, a live performance, a demo, an interview. The same recording can later appear on several albums and editions. Open a library and choose **Recordings**.

## Finding a recording

Search matches the title, or any name of any performer credited on the recording, ignoring case, accents and punctuation: searching for a performer's birth name or alias finds the recordings that credit that performer. Results show the credit line, the kind, the recording date and the duration, 25 to a page.

## Creating and editing

Create a recording with its title, its kind (studio, live, demo, rehearsal, alternate take, remix, edit, session, interview, spoken word) and, optionally, a version such as "live at Newport". The editor then takes:

- **Recorded on or from** and **Recorded until**: a year, a year and month, or a full date (`1959`, `1959-03`, `1959-03-02`); use both for sessions over several days.
- **Venue or studio**.
- **Duration** as `m:ss` or `h:mm:ss`, with optional milliseconds (`9:22`, `1:02:03`, `9:22.347`).
- **Notes**.

**Delete recording** asks for confirmation, then removes the recording with its credits.

## Credits

Credits are an ordered list. Each credit names a performer and a role:

| Role | Use |
|---|---|
| Primary artist, Featured artist | Whose recording it is, and guests |
| Soloist, Accompanist | A soloist with an orchestra; a pianist accompanying a singer |
| Conductor, Orchestra, Choir, Ensemble | Classical and choral forces |
| Composer, Lyricist, Arranger | Who wrote or arranged the piece |
| Producer, Remixer | Production |

To add a credit, find the performer, choose it and a role, and choose **Add credit**. In the list, each credit can then be given:

- **Credited as**: which of the performer's names was printed. Left at the preferred name, the credit follows the performer's preferred name if it changes later.
- **Joined to the next credit by**: the text between this credit and the next, used exactly as typed, spaces included: `" & "`, `" feat. "`, `" with "`.
- **Instruments or parts**, separated by commas.

**Move up**, **Move down** and **Remove** change the list. Nothing is stored until **Save credits**; **Discard changes** returns to the stored list.

## The credit line

The line at the top of the credits, and in search results, is made from the performing credits in order (primary, featured, soloist, accompanist, conductor, orchestra, choir, ensemble), each under the name it was credited as and followed by its join text. A credit without join text is followed by a comma; the last credit's join text is not shown. Writers and producers are credited but are not part of the line.

| Credits | Line |
|---|---|
| Paul Simon (primary, `" & "`), Art Garfunkel (primary) | Paul Simon & Art Garfunkel |
| Santana (primary, `" feat. "`), Rob Thomas (featured) | Santana feat. Rob Thomas |
| Itzhak Perlman (soloist), Chicago Symphony Orchestra (orchestra), Carlo Maria Giulini (conductor) | Itzhak Perlman, Chicago Symphony Orchestra, Carlo Maria Giulini |
